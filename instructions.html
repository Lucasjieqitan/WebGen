YOUR WEBGEN GUIDE

How to start creating

Three simple stages take you from a first idea to a website you can be proud of.

THE WEBGEN PROCESS 01 / 03
✦

STEP ONE

Describe your idea

Start by thinking about the business or project your website is for. Add its name, what it does, who it helps and the sections you want visitors to see.

Tip: A clear description gives you a stronger starting point.

STEP TWO

Efficient construction

Simply choose your presets such as colour themes, style, and add your logo. Then, hit generate.

Tip: Use the HEX colour spectrum to choose EXACTLY what colour you want for your brand.

✦

STEP THREE

Impressive product

Be amazed! Within seconds, the finished product will be before your very eyes. If it doesn't appeal to you, you can always regenerate it.

Tip: Test your website on a phone or tablet as well as a computer.
