Introduction

Welcome to WebGen. This page explains how privacy should be considered when using this website and its website-generator prototype.

Important: This is a student project demonstration and the privacy wording is a draft. It must be checked and updated to accurately describe the final website before collecting personal information or making the service publicly available.

What information do we collect?

The current demonstration may ask visitors to enter business details and choose design preferences to create a website preview. Do not enter passwords, payment details, private contact information or other sensitive personal information into a prototype.

How do we use your information?

Information entered into the generator is intended to help create and display a website preview. The final version of this notice should explain exactly what information is processed, whether it is stored, which services receive it and how long it is kept.

Do we make updates to this notice?

This notice may be updated as WebGen develops. Any published version should include an accurate update date and a contact method that the project owner can monitor.
